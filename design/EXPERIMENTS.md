# Visual Language Lab

Concept exploration, not production. The institutional model (doctrine, lawbook,
archetypes, lifecycles) is distinctive; the visual language still reads as a
disciplined enterprise admin application. These experiments search for a visual
metaphor as distinctive as the philosophy.

## Jurisdiction

Experiments live in `src/labs/` under the `#/lab/*` hash namespace. They are
deliberately OUTSIDE the doctrine court's jurisdiction: the per-page checks
scope to `src/pages/`, labs carry no PAGE_CLASSIFICATION, no route-map row, no
nav entry, and do not count toward any coverage or composition metric. They may
break density budgets, motion rules, and the Bindu rule while exploring.

Promotion is the only door back: an experiment that earns production status is
rebuilt as a page under full doctrine — classification, archetype row, route-map
mark, budget compliance. Nothing moves from lab to production by relocation.

## Register

| Lab | Route | Question it explores |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutional Ledger | #/lab/ledger | Employee record as continuous constitutional record — git history + court record + institutional biography, not tabs |
| Observatory | #/lab/observatory | Executive surface as instrument room (mission control / central bank), not KPI grid; includes atmospheric pressure states (watch condition compresses margins and typography) |
| Living Topology | #/lab/topology | Team attendance as territory map — zones expand with health, contract under risk; the institution literally changes shape |
| Modern Pulse | #/lab/modern | Ultra-modern youthful probe — gradients, glassmorphism, glow, spring hover, count-up numbers, staggered entrances. Every doctrine rule intentionally broken; the question is whether the product should move here wholly, per persona, or not at all |
