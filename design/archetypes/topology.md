# Archetype: Graph / Topology

ID: `topology`
Question: *What is the structural shape of the organization?*

Maps of institutional structure: hierarchies, calibration grids, succession lattices.
A topology screen is a Map in the information-class sense — it is never counted as a
KPI, and it owns the canvas the way a Builder's artifact does.

## Zones

1. **Map header** — what structure, as of when, source projection in mono.
2. **The map** (dominant) — one MapSurface: org chart, 9-box grid, workforce graph,
   calibration matrix. Cells/nodes carry mono labels and small monogram markers.
   Axes labeled in mono-label at the frame edge.
3. **Reading rail** — selected node/cell detail: who is here, why, with links to
   Record Detail.
4. **Structural findings** — 1–2 RiskCards or SignalCards derived from shape: span
   of control outliers, empty succession cells, fragile coverage.
5. **Ledger strip** — snapshot id, computation time.

## Classification profile

Maps 1 (the point of the screen), Signals 1–3 (derived structural metrics), Risks
0–2 (structural fragilities), Narratives 0–1 (Buddhi reading the shape).

## Bindu

Zero or one: a structural act ("Convene Calibration", "Activate Succession Plan").
Navigation through the map is never copper.

## Component manifest

`MapSurface`, `RiskCard`, `SignalCard`, `NarrativePlate`, `LedgerStrip`, `BinduButton`.

## Used by

Org Chart & Workforce Graph, Bench Strength & 9-Box, Succession Workspace
(calibration map), Talent Heatmap, Calibration Board.

## Anti-patterns

- Decorative graph physics. Nodes do not float, spring, or bounce (DOCTRINE §5b).
- Color-coding more than three states. Topology reads by position first.
- Treating the map as a chart. A 9-box is structure, not a metric visualization.
