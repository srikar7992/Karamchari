# Archetype: Intelligence

ID: `intelligence`
Question: *What does the institution recommend, and why?*

The Buddhi surface. Intelligence screens present machine-derived judgment for human
ratification. Their entire credibility rests on showing reasoning: a recommendation
without its evidence chain is noise and does not ship.

## Zones

1. **Posture header** — the institution's current stance in one Newsreader sentence
   ("Buddhi observes elevated attrition pressure in two clusters; posture: watchful").
2. **Recommendation plates** — each recommendation as a NarrativePlate pair:
   the claim (serif prose) + the evidence (tabular facts with source projections in
   mono). Confidence stated numerically, never as color drama.
3. **Risk board** — RiskCards for detected risks, severity by hairline tone.
4. **Scenario / simulation strip** — when the screen runs models: parameters in,
   projected deltas out, presented as a comparison table.
5. **Disposition strip** — Acknowledge / Dismiss per recommendation, ink. Dismissal
   requires a reason; both land in the audit trail.

## Classification profile

Signals 1–3, Risks up to persona budget (EXEC: 5), Narratives 1 (the posture or the
digest — multiple recommendation plates are one narrative class instance per screen
budget accounting only if separately argued), Maps 0–1.

## Bindu

One: adopting the institution's recommendation as decision ("Commit Intervention",
"Adopt Scenario"). Acknowledge/dismiss are ink.

## Component manifest

`NarrativePlate`, `RiskCard`, `SignalCard`, `InstitutionalTable`, `BinduButton`,
`LedgerStrip`.

## Used by

Strategy Workspace, Decision Center, Executive Digest, Risk & Recommendation Board,
Optimization Console, Scenario & Simulation Lab, Team Risk Panel, Intervention
Tracker (registry-leaning, intelligence-toned).

## Anti-patterns

- Oracle voice. Buddhi cites projections; it never says "AI thinks".
- Confidence theater (gauges, glowing scores). A number and its basis.
- Recommendations that cannot be dismissed. Human authority is the final layer.
